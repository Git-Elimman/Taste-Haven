OUR STORY

About Taste Haven

Taste Haven is a Nigerian restaurant created from a love for good food warm hospitality, and memorable dining experiences.

We bring together the richness of Nigerian cuisines and a modern dining experience, giving our customers a place to enjoy carefully prepared meals in a welcoming environment.

WAHT WE BELIEVE

Food Made With Care

At Taste Haven, we believe food should be more than something you simply eat. It should bring people together, create memories and give you something to look forward to.

From traditional Nigerian favourites to carefully prepared contemporary dishes, every meal is prepared with attention to quality, flavour, and presentation.

OUR VALUES

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Every meal and every interaction at Taste Haven is guided by a few simple principles.

Quality

We pay attention to the ingredients, preparation and presentation that go into every meal.
